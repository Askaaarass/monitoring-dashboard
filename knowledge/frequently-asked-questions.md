# Frequently asked questions

## Does the dashboard show live hardware data?

No. Current website readings are labelled DEMO and are simulated. Connecting the ESP32 is a future development step.

## What does the ESP32 do?

It is the planned controller for collecting sensor readings and coordinating the hardware. The live firmware-to-website telemetry path is not yet implemented.

## How is plant health assessed?

Plant photographs and computer-vision analysis are planned. The site does not currently provide a validated automated plant-health result.

## Can I rely on the demonstration thresholds?

They are configurable website demonstration thresholds, not verified calibration or care recommendations for a physical installation.
